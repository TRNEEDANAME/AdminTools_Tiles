description=[h4]Tile Swap[/h4]
description=
description=Create a set of rules (preset) that allows you to swap tiles around (grass floor -> road), add overlays / tiles (add grass tiles to wood floors) / remove tiles and a lot of such features (with % chance)
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Include a burnt / 10Y later (light), True 10Y later (destroy some wall tiles & more vegetation), Burnt and Bloody tile preset
description=[*]Share your preset as a string
description=[*]Undo your changes (up to 20 swaps)
description=[*]Zone Editor (allow you to swap the whole map) with a "load" feature (tp your character every few tiles to load them)
description=[*]Floor select (can go from -32 to +32 floors)
description=[*]The tile swap window support tile search (for the swapping & swapped tiles)
description=[*]Tile Picker for the tile to swap from and to.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h4]Tile Remover[/h4]
description=
description=A standalone tile remover that works like the tile copy, allows you to remove a set of tiles (kinda superseeded by Tile Swap)
description=
description=[h4]Tile Builder[/h4]
description=
description=The Brush Tool window, but drag in the world to build a line / area of the picked tile
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Walls, fences & window walls turn to follow the line
description=[*]Undo Last takes a drag back
description=[/list]

description=A "Building" mode in the Zone Editor (in the right-click menu in single player)
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Draw a room, pick a floor and / or a wall tile, and build the floor and the walls around it (corner & pillar included) on every floor of the range
description=[*]Copy House / Paste House

description=[h3] Loot[/h3]
description=
description=[h4]Loot Filler[/h4]
description=
description=Select a zone and add loots
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Select floors
description=[*]Select if the loot comes from your inventory or is rerolled from the vanilla system (container type & location), and if so, is the loot added or deleted -> rerolled
description=[*]Select the type of fill logic used
description=[*]Tick categories: filters your inventory list, limits rerolled loot to them, and "Replace" only empties those
description=[*]Random items by container type: scan the zone's container types, give each one its categories and an amount, and every container of that type gets that many random items
description=[*]Limit what gets added with an item list: a whitelist (only those items) or a blacklist (never those items). Lists are edited in game, saved, and shared as a string (Export / Import). Built-ins: Military, Gunworks ammo, Survivor
description=[*]Server-wide blacklist in the sandbox options ("Loot Filler blacklist"), applied on top of any list
description=[/list]
